## Notes on sourcing and further verification

- Entries [1]–[2] are standard, long-established references in Oromo/
  Cushitic lexicography; [1] in particular is widely cited in the Oromo
  linguistics literature. Neither was accessed in full text during this
  session — cite the print/archival edition directly when drafting the
  manuscript, and confirm page ranges against a library catalog record
  (e.g. WorldCat) before submission.
- [3]–[5] were retrieved and their bibliographic data extracted directly
  from the publisher/repository page.
- [6]–[8] are the three Afaan Oromo NLP spelling/grammar-correction
  systems located during literature search; **no broader systematic
  search of this sub-literature has been done yet** — before manuscript
  submission, run a fuller search (Google Scholar, ACL Anthology,
  Semantic Scholar) for additional related work (e.g. the Wikipedia-
  corpus context-sensitive checker at
  https://journals.ju.edu.et/index.php/gadaa/article/view/2019 and the
  Ethiopian-languages misspelling system at
  https://journalofcloudcomputing.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s13677-022-00299-1
  turned up in search but were not yet verified/fetched for exact
  citation data).
- [10]–[12] are foundational, universally-cited references in the
  spelling-correction literature; verify exact page numbers against a
  library database before submission, as they were cited from
  well-established prior knowledge rather than a freshly fetched source
  in this session.
- Every citation used to justify a *specific orthographic rule* in
  `docs/linguistic-background.md` ([1]–[3]) traces to a verified source
  above — do not add further orthographic claims to that document
  without a matching verified entry here.
